Life Lead Distribution - Windows Key Setup 0.2.0
Windows 10/11 x64. Portable trial, not an installer.

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder on your computer.
2. Open Life-Lead-Distribution-Trial.exe from that folder.
3. Read the trial notice and choose Open my portal when ready.
4. Sign in with your existing account and complete MFA if required.
Keep the EXE with all accompanying files. Do not run it inside the ZIP.

The existing portal interface and workflows are bundled locally, including
the approved LLD logo. The sign-in library is bundled, not loaded from a CDN.

IMPORTANT
This connects to your LIVE account. Edits, uploads and deletions affect real
leads. It is not a sample database. New field encryption is NOT enabled.
Use Encryption > Master key preparation (or Ctrl+Shift+E) to prepare a local
key, save its encrypted recovery file, and reopen it to verify recovery.
This does not enroll the key or activate production encryption.
Do not share your password or recovery file in chat.

This trial is UNSIGNED. Windows may flag an unknown publisher or block it.
Do not disable Windows security. If blocked, report the exact message so a
signed release or another trial approach can be arranged.

No automatic updates. This is an evaluation build, not a production release.
Do not distribute to the whole team yet. Packaged on Linux; Windows launch,
real sign-in and end-to-end lead operations have NOT been tested here.
Auth sessions still use the portal's browser storage in a separate app
profile. They have not been moved into OS credential storage.
Mac, iPhone/iPad and Android builds are not included yet.

TRY FIRST
Compare screens, sidebar and lead scrolling with your website. Remember
that testing write operations changes your real data. Report differences.

GO BACK
Close the app and continue using the website. No website or database
migration is required. Sign out before removing the trial. Delete the
portable folder to remove the app. Its separate local profile remains at
%APPDATA%\Life Lead Distribution Trial until removed from your computer.
